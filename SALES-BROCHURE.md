## Key Features

### Customer Portal
- **Online Service Ordering** - Customers browse and order services directly
- **Secure Payment Processing** - Integrated mobile money & card payments (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel, Visa/Mastercard)
- **Project Tracking** - Real-time status updates for customers
- **Customer Dashboard** - Self-service portal for order history and communication

### Project Management
- **End-to-End Tracking** - From order to delivery
- **Task Assignment** - Assign work to team members with deadlines
- **Progress Monitoring** - Visual status tracking
- **Approval Workflows** - Multi-level review process

### Accounting & Finance
- **Revenue Tracking** - Automatic recording from payments
- **Expense Management** - Record and approve expenses
- **Asset Management** - Track company assets with depreciation
- **Liability Tracking** - Monitor debts and obligations
- **Financial Reports** - Profit & Loss, Trial Balance
- **Petty Cash** - Daily cash management

### HR & Staff Management
- **Staff Directory** - Complete employee database
- **Leave Management** - Applications and approvals
- **Salary Records** - Payroll tracking and history
- **Role-Based Access** - 9 permission levels

### Communication
- **Internal Messaging** - Staff-to-staff communication
- **Automated Notifications** - Payment alerts, task assignments, project updates
- **Customer Support** - Built-in messaging with customers
